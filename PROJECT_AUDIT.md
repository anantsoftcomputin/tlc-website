# TLC Holidays project audit

## Repository

The supplied workspace was empty. This build therefore establishes a new Next.js 16 application rather than migrating an existing codebase.

## Legacy website findings (reviewed 1 September 2026)

Verified public content includes the TLC Travels/Holidays name, Kanpur office at 11/26, 1-B, Ground Floor, Karmin Apartments, Suter Ganj, phone numbers 0512-2550055 and 89488 88873–75 / 9919999199, and info@tlcholidays.in. Published services include air ticket booking, accommodation, visa assistance, Euro Rail tickets, packages, and cruises. Published destinations include Thailand, Germany, Bhutan, Ireland, Argentina, South Africa, Dubai, Singapore, and Australia.

The home page includes three credible named testimonials (Rishab Vora, Sailesh Jha and Rubina Malik). One testimonial attributed to “Robert Hanson” contains obvious placeholder copy and has not been migrated. The claim of presence in 180 countries is not independently supported and has not been reused.

## Build scope

This implementation prioritises the Phase 1–3 customer experience: premium design foundation, homepage, destination discovery, trip listing/detail, saved trips, holiday styles, Plan My Trip, context-aware WhatsApp, verified company content, SEO basics, and persistence interfaces. Firebase rules and deployment foundations are included; production Firebase, email, AI and admin integrations require project credentials and approved content.
